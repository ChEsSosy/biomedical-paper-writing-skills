# Cell Experiment Profile

Use for cell lines, primary cells, organoids, ex-vivo cell systems, and cell-based assays.

Check:

- cell identity, source, model context, treatment or perturbation, dose, and time when reported;
- experimental unit: well, dish, culture, donor, differentiation batch, or independent experiment;
- biological versus technical replicates and whether repeated measurements are independent;
- vehicle, untreated, mock, non-targeting, positive, rescue, or matched controls as applicable;
- endpoint identity, assay dynamic range, normalization, viability, and batch limitations when supplied;
- whether multiple cell models or donors support generality.

Claim ceilings:

- a cell-system result supports the tested in-vitro or ex-vivo context, not organismal, clinical, or population relevance;
- one cell line or donor does not establish generality;
- target perturbation plus phenotype does not by itself establish a complete mechanism;
- technical replicates do not substitute for independent biological replicates;
- an assay readout does not establish a broader phenotype beyond what it measures.

Missing authentication, contamination testing, donor metadata, or replicate definitions should be marked not reported when relevant, not assumed absent or present.
