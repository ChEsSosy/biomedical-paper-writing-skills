# Additional Evidence Modalities

Use the applicable subsection for `omics`, `imaging`, `clinical_human`, or `other` labels. Combine it with computational, cell, molecular, or animal profiles when evidence is multi-label.

## Omics

Record sample identity, biological unit, batch structure, preprocessing, normalization, filtering, multiplicity, feature definition, and validation level. Differential features or enrichment do not by themselves establish mechanism, cell-intrinsic effects, or pathway activity.

## Imaging

Record specimen and field identity, acquisition unit, sampling hierarchy, segmentation/quantification method, blinding when reported, and whether images are representative or quantitative. Fields, objects, or repeated images from one specimen are not automatically independent biological replicates.

## Clinical/human

Record cohort, design, eligibility, exposure/intervention, outcome, follow-up, missingness, confounding, and analysis population when reported. Observational associations are not treatment effects; retrospective or single-cohort performance is not prospective clinical utility; subgroup findings require their actual design and uncertainty.

## Other

Do not force an unfamiliar assay into a known profile. Record the declared unit, controls, endpoint, statistics, limitations, and explicit not-assessed fields. Ask the author for a domain-specific boundary when the claim ceiling cannot be determined safely.
