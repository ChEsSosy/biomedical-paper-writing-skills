# Computational Evidence Profile

Use for bioinformatics, statistical modelling, simulation, prediction, enrichment, docking, and other dry-lab evidence.

Check:

- dataset, cohort, split, preprocessing, filtering, and feature identities;
- unit of analysis and whether observations are independent;
- train/validation/test separation and leakage risk when modelling;
- comparator, baseline, ablation, sensitivity analysis, and external validation when applicable;
- multiplicity, uncertainty, effect size, and statistics locators;
- whether the result is descriptive, associative, predictive, or mechanistic.

Claim ceilings:

- enrichment, trajectory, communication, network, or attribution outputs are analytic interpretations, not direct pathway activity or mechanism;
- prediction performance is not clinical utility without an appropriate clinical evaluation;
- docking or simulation is not experimental binding, biochemical activity, or in-vivo efficacy;
- an association in one dataset does not establish causality or cross-cohort generality;
- an internal validation result is not independent external validation.

Retain dataset shift, leakage, batch, confounding, missingness, and model-calibration risks when supported by the supplied metadata. Do not invent them as facts.
