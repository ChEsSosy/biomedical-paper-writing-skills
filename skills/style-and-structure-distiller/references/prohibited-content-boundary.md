# Prohibited Content Boundary

Phase 3.1 may retain source-derived structure, counts, locators, distributions, and short reference tokens such as `Fig. 2a–c`. It may not convert scientific propositions into reusable writing guidance.

## Allowed reusable findings

- sentence and paragraph distributions;
- raw/canonical heading hierarchy and section architecture;
- marker, citation, and Figure-reference placement distributions;
- many-to-many source-reference links;
- parser QC, corpus balance, and feature-support evidence.

## Prohibited findings

- hypotheses, mechanisms, conclusions, novelty, or favored targets;
- scientific evidence-role or rhetorical-role labels;
- author/lab positions, research priorities, distinctive voice, personality, or worldview;
- unique phrases, long quotations, captions, Figure panels, or layouts;
- claims that a deterministic parser understands manuscript intent.

## Neutralization test

A reusable Phase 3.1 finding must remain meaningful after all scientific nouns and propositions are replaced with neutral placeholders. It must be expressible as source structure or deterministic measurement and usable without importing source-paper facts.

If a proposed field fails this test, omit it and record an appropriate warning rather than paraphrasing it into a style rule.

The generated `style-profile` records a recursive prohibited-field audit over every in-memory artifact, including scan counts and exact finding paths. A passing flag is therefore an observed audit result, not a fixed declaration.
